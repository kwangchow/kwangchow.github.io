Experience

I've spent 30 years making games and playful things: Grand Theft Auto at DMA Design, mobile before smartphones, interactive TV before app stores, then browser, social and whatever came next.

Along the way I've shipped 100+ games, led studios' product and production work, built an R&D centre and co-written a book. The thread through all of it is helping people make something worth playing - and giving it a chance to find its audience.

How I work

At Denki we called the role Product Architect: shaping what a game becomes and building the process that gets it there. I like being close enough to the work to prototype an idea, question a feature or play through the latest build.

I translate a founder's intent into priorities a team can act on, test assumptions early and keep scope, quality and commercial goals in view. I've spent more than 20 years managing distributed teams and taking responsibility for budgets, capacity and delivery.

Executive Director of InGAME | Abertay University

2018-2024

Established and led a £5.3m UK government-funded research and innovation centre for games and immersive media. I was accountable for the programme, budget and team of 12, reporting to a board drawn from industry, universities and the public sector.

We brought researchers and studios together to test ideas, develop new products and explore markets. InGAME delivered 177 industry collaborations and upskilled 2,283 professionals. A 2023 independent assessment projected £84.7m in gross value added to Scotland's economy over ten years, representing a 16× return on public investment.

Producer / Product Architect | Denki
